### Summary of Proprietary Trading Model Using Deep Order Flow Analysis

This video presents a **proprietary trading model** developed after extensive research, focusing on granular **order flow data** to identify where volume moves price with minimal resistance—essentially the **path of least resistance** in the market. The model is designed to provide traders with high-probability trading areas by analyzing the relationship between volume and price movement, which the presenter integrates into daily trading routines and quantitative validation.

### Core Concepts and Methodology

- **Volume Spread Analysis (VSA)** is the foundational study behind the model. It examines the interplay between volume and price movement to detect market dynamics such as absorption, effort, and result.
- The model identifies **effort versus result**:
  - **Effort**: The volume or number of orders entering the market.
  - **Result**: The impact these orders have on price movement.
- Key insight: When there is **high volume but little or no price movement**, it signals **absorption** by passive participants, indicating a potential reversal or significant level.
- Conversely, when **low effort leads to strong price movement**, it suggests that one side (buyers or sellers) has gained a directional edge.
- The approach filters noise in price and volume data by combining them with **order flow granularity**, generating **triggers** for potential trade execution.
- This enables traders to:
  - Define precise entry areas.
  - Set clear stop losses aligned with the directional bias.
  - Manage trades smartly by recognizing when to take profits or set stop losses to protect gains.

### Practical Application and Trade Management

- The model pairs well with **statistical frameworks like Initial Balance Volume (IVB)** and **profile framing** to define daily directional bias.
- Traders use the model to identify:
  - Areas where buying or selling pressure dominates.
  - Momentum setups, where price closes above or below key zones without retesting.
- Multiple profit targets can be employed:
  - Example: Take 50% profit at a first target and leave the rest as a runner.
- The model emphasizes trading **in alignment with the day's bias**, avoiding counter-trend setups to enhance probability.
- The presenter highlights managing risk with **small stop losses** for precision and acknowledges that some setups will be unfulfilled, which is an expected part of the strategy.

### Trading Logic and Theoretical Foundation

- The model relies on the **Law of Effort and Result**:
  - Effort (volume) must correspond to the result (price movement) for continuation.
  - A mismatch (high effort, low result) indicates absorption and potential reversal.
  - Equal effort from buyers and sellers but skewed result (e.g., sellers absorbed) signals directional bias.
- The model’s **proprietary script** automates this logic by analyzing order flow granularly, providing actionable signals and minimizing noise.

- The trader preferred **conservative profit-taking** over holding for maximum potential gains ($40,000 to $60,000 estimated) to secure consistent performance.
- Key market behaviors such as **buyers’ control, failed sell attempts, and consolidation areas** were identified and used for trade management.
- The trader emphasized **risk-reward balance** and **quick reaction to market signals** as critical to success.

### Core Concepts & Techniques Highlighted

- **Order Flow Analysis:** Understanding buyer and seller aggression through volume and trade absorption.
- **Failed Auction Concept:** Selling pressure failing to push prices lower indicates buyer strength.
- **Break-even Stops:** Moving stops to entry price to neutralize risk quickly.
- **Scaling In/Out:** Gradually increasing or decreasing position size based on market confirmation.
- **Risk-to-Reward Management:** Balancing potential profits against limited risk exposure.
- **Protection Levels:** Using key pivot points as stop-loss or take-profit levels.
- **Conservative Profit Taking:** Prioritizing consistent wins over chasing maximal profits.

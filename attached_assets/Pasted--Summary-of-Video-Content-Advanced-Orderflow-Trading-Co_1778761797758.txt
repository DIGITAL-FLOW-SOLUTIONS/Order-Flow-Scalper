### Summary of Video Content: Advanced Orderflow Trading Concepts

The video presents an expert trader's approach to scalping using **orderflow charts**, a specialized tool that reveals the interaction between buyers and sellers beyond traditional price candlestick charts. The speaker highlights how these charts improve trading decisions by filtering out noise from small traders and focusing on the activity of “smart money” or informed market participants.

---

### Key Concepts and Insights

- **Orderflow Charts vs. Candlestick Charts**  
  - Traditional candlestick charts show price movement but lack detailed information on the volume and aggressiveness of buyers and sellers.  
  - Orderflow charts display **delta values** (difference between buying and selling volume), total traded volume, and color-coded aggression levels of market participants, offering a **more granular insight into market dynamics**.

- **Market Structure and Breakouts**  
  - The video shows an example where buyers defend a price level ($24,800) while sellers defend a higher range ($24,825–$24,826).  
  - Price action traders typically wait for breakout confirmation, but orderflow traders gain an advantage by observing **order absorption** and the **aggressiveness of buyers/sellers** before the breakout.  
  - A key example demonstrates **buyers’ aggression being absorbed by sellers**, revealing potential breakout points sooner than standard price action signals.

- **Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Void**  
  - A **fair value gap** is defined as an imbalance in price action where volume did not transact evenly within a price range.  
  - Unlike price action traders who identify FVGs by price candles alone, the speaker emphasizes the importance of analyzing **volume profiles inside the candle** to locate the actual FVG.  
  - The **fixed volume profile** shows the lowest-volume areas inside a value range as the true FVG, often a very narrow price level rather than a broad area.  
  - These FVGs tend to act as strong levels of price rejection and should be incorporated into trading strategies for **higher precision and clarity**.

- **Volume Spread Analysis and Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD)**  
  - The speaker introduces **volume spread analysis** as a way to assess market pressure by comparing cumulative volume delta and price movement.  
  - CVD is a critical indicator reflecting the net difference between aggressive buying and selling.  
  - Examples show how **absorption patterns** occur when sellers attempt to push price down but are absorbed by passive buyers, evidenced by lower lows in CVD without corresponding price declines.  
  - This mismatch between price and volume signals **failed breakouts** or trend reversals, providing high-probability trade setups.  
  - Traders can use CVD to identify whether the market is in **accumulation (buying pressure)** or **distribution (selling pressure)**, crucial during price compression phases.

### Core Techniques and Practical Applications

- Use **orderflow charts** to monitor **buyer/seller aggression and absorption** rather than relying solely on price breakout confirmation.
- Identify **fair value gaps** by analyzing volume profiles within candles to find precise imbalance zones that act as rejection or support/resistance levels.
- Apply **cumulative volume delta** to detect hidden buying or selling pressure during price compressions or apparent breakouts.
- Recognize **absorption patterns** where aggressive sellers or buyers fail to move the price, signaling potential reversals or continuation with higher confidence.
- Integrate these tools for **higher precision trading**, gaining an edge over price action-only traders by understanding the underlying market mechanics.
